About MedInsight

MedInsight is a clinical AI assistant designed to bridge the gap between complex medical information and everyday understanding. Our platform helps patients comprehend their prescriptions accurately and enables healthcare professionals to make more informed decisions through AI-powered chest X-ray analysis and clinical Q&A tools. Built with privacy and security at its core, MedInsight ensures that all medical data is handled with the utmost confidentiality while delivering accurate, reliable results to support better healthcare outcomes.
